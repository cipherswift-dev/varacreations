What we stitch

One studio, every kind of embroidery

Simple or intricate, one piece or fifty — tell us what you have in mind and we'll suggest the right threadwork, placement and finish.

Saree blouse embroidery

Our speciality. Aari and maggam-style work, zari, beads, mirror and threadwork — matched to your saree, for weddings, festivals and everyday elegance.

School uniforms & shirts

Names, class labels and school logos stitched neatly on shirts — durable embroidery that survives daily wear and every wash.

Team & group orders

Sports teams, event crews, office staff — one logo across many garments, with consistent placement and colour on every piece.

Personalised gifts

Monograms and names on kerchiefs, towels, baby wraps and keepsakes — small stitches that make a gift feel truly personal.

All ages, all styles

From a child's frock to a grandmother's shawl — playful motifs, florals, traditional patterns or clean minimal lines, sized for anyone.

Classes & workshops

Learn the craft with us — beginner-friendly sessions at our home studio for anyone who wants to pick up needle and thread.
